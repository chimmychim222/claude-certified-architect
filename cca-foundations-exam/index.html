What Is the CCA Foundations Exam?

The Claude Certified Architect (CCA) Foundations exam is Anthropic's official certification for professionals who design, build, and deploy systems powered by Claude. It validates your ability to architect production-grade AI applications across five core domains.

Our full-length CCA Foundations exam simulation replicates the exam's published testing conditions — timed at 120 minutes with 60 scenario-based questions drawn from our 400-question bank and weighted across all five domains at the published exam guide's weightings.

The Five CCA Exam Domains

Each domain carries a specific weight in the overall score, matching the distribution used in the CCA certification:

1. Agentic Architecture & Orchestration (27%)

Design patterns for autonomous AI agents, orchestration strategies, multi-agent systems, and reliability patterns like retry loops.

2. Claude Code Configuration & Workflows (20%)

CLAUDE.md files, project configuration, slash commands, permission models, and MCP server integration within Claude Code workflows.

3. Prompt Engineering & Structured Output (20%)

System prompt design, chain-of-thought techniques, few-shot patterns, role prompting, and structured prompt construction.

4. Tool Design & MCP Integration (18%)

Model Context Protocol server architecture, tool schema design, resource management, transport layers, and security considerations for tool-use patterns.

5. Context Management & Reliability (15%)

Token budget strategies, caching with ephemeral and persistent patterns, multi-turn conversation design, and context optimization.

For a deeper walkthrough of each domain — including worked scenarios and the traps that catch candidates — see our domain-by-domain breakdown, or jump straight to Domain 1: Agentic Architecture & Orchestration, the largest section of the exam.

How Our Simulation Compares to the Real Exam

Every question in our practice exam is scenario-based — you will not find simple recall questions. Instead, you will be presented with real-world architecture decisions, debugging scenarios, and system design challenges that test your ability to apply knowledge under pressure.

The domain weighting aligns with the published CCA distribution, so your practice score is a reliable predictor of your actual exam performance.

Additional Practice Modes
