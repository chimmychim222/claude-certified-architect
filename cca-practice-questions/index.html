Sample Questions: How They Work

When an enrolled user starts a practice session, questions are drawn from the full 400-question bank using a Fisher-Yates shuffle, so every session presents a different order. Without an account, Quick Sprint and the first five questions of a Focused Session draw from a fixed 55-question sample of the bank. You can choose from five practice modes:

  • Quick Sprint — 10 random questions in 20 minutes; free without an account, drawn from the 55-question sample
  • Focused Session — 20 random questions in 40 minutes; the first five need no account and come from the 55-question sample
  • Deep Practice — 30 questions from the full 400-question bank across all domains, in 60 minutes
  • Domain Drill — 20 questions from a single exam domain in 40 minutes; 109 questions in Agentic Architecture, 80 in Claude Code Configuration, 79 in Prompt Engineering, 72 in Tool Design & MCP, 60 in Context Management
  • Full Exam — 60 questions from the full 400-question bank in 120 minutes, drawn at the published exam guide's domain weighting: 16 Agentic, 12 Claude Code, 12 Prompt Engineering, 11 Tool Design, 9 Context Management
